# How reports and receipts are recorded

## Evidence index

For each item record: item ID; source and acquisition method; received/captured time and timezone; private original path; SHA-256 if available; redacted copy; observation; inference; uncertainty. Hashes show byte identity, not authenticity. Preserve message dates as supplied when locale is ambiguous.

## Report template

Subject: Suspected impersonation involving [domain or brand] — investigation request

I am reporting suspected [recruitment/brand/phishing] impersonation. On [date and timezone], [observed channel] supplied [relevant URL] and requested [exact action]. I have not independently established the sender's identity or affiliation.

Observed evidence: [brief numbered facts and source timestamps]. Relevant infrastructure: [verified registrar/provider and record source]. The evidence suggests [qualified concern]; please investigate under your applicable abuse process. [Minimal redacted attachments or excerpts]. Please acknowledge receipt or advise the correct reporting channel.

Do not assert that no payment, registration or disclosure occurred unless the user confirms it. State only what the investigation actually did.

Allowed states: prepared; submission unconfirmed; submitted; acknowledged; awaiting review; actioned; blocked. Keep previous attempts and state transitions with timestamps. A platform block and an abuse-report submission are different events.

## Singapore route

Check ScamShield's official website for the current app and submission guidance. Record whether the report covers a message, number or website, the evidence actually attached, displayed confirmation and any reference number. If device authentication is required, preserve the draft and request the device action. Do not infer a receipt from navigation, a filled form or a tap alone. Use the official police reporting route separately when appropriate.

## Before distribution

Inspect all filenames, text, image metadata and archive members. Exclude originals and receipts entirely; redaction is for authorised reports, not a reason to publish case evidence. Use reserved example.com or .invalid domains in reusable examples. Verify relative links remain inside the bundle and no symlinks escape it.
